Myriad Swords privacy policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Myriad Swords (万剑归宗：一剑万剑) is developed by 简单极限游戏. It is an offline, single-player game. No registration or login is required to play.

Personal information and services

The game does not collect, upload, share or sell personal information. It has no advertising, analytics, payment or account-login services. It does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Illustrations and portions of the music and sound effects were made with AI assistance. The game does not provide an AI generation service or send player data to an AI service.

Data stored on your device

Settings, play counts and game records are stored locally on your device. You can delete these records by clearing the app's data in your system settings or uninstalling the game. Whether the operating system backs up or restores app data depends on your system settings.

Saving, sharing and external links

When you choose to save or share a painting, the game creates an image file and uses the device's system sharing tools, or downloads the file in the web version. You choose where to share it. The selected app handles the file under its own privacy policy. The game does not upload paintings on its own.

Community and review links are optional. Opening one takes you to an external service, such as TapTap or QQ. Your use of that service is covered by its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from children. Minors should play with a guardian's guidance and take appropriate breaks.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact zwyijian@gmail.com.

Policy updates

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date, and provide the updated explanation in the game.
